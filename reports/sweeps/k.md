## Reading this curve

Expectation, stated before the numbers: recall@k rises with depth until the retriever's misses are the only thing left; past that point the curve is flat and the extra candidates only cost context tokens, so the depth saturates where recall@k and citation recall stop moving.

Saturation is read off the recall@k and citation-precision columns: the first point after which neither moves by more than the run-to-run noise (ADR-0012: a rerun reproduces its metrics through the cache) is where the swept parameter stops buying anything.
